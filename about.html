Who we are

A legacy of quality and great taste

In 1962, the first generation of our founding family began trading potatoes and onions. The second generation grew the business, and in November 2015 the third generation founded RT FOOD to make French fries, burger patties, cheese poppers and more.

Within five years RT FOOD became a trusted B2B partner to leading global QSR brands. Proudly Made in India, we now ship to 45+ countries — an Indian company with global standards — and we’re bringing the same quality to homes through retail and online.
